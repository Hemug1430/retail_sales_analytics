## 3. Strategic Recommendations
1. **Margin-Protected Promotional Thresholds:** Maintain the disciplined 1.14% effective discount rate and 19.15% gross margin by tying discounts to minimum basket thresholds (>65,000) in Home & Kitchen and Grocery.
2. **Central & East Regional Expansion:** Prioritize store network and logistics investment in Central (4.42%) and East (15.44%) to complement the mature South/West/North core (80.14% combined share).
3. **Omnichannel & Return Optimization:** Scale Online share (24.89%) via buy-online-pickup-in-store (BOPIS) while reducing the 3.29B refund outflow through pre-purchase sizing/spec guidance.
