## 2. Insights

### 2.1 Region and format
- **South is the largest region** with 34.4% of revenue (584.7 M), then West 26.8%, North 25.3% and East 13.6%.
  East being the weakest region is consistent with the lower East baseline (index 0.88) in Document 03.
- **Mall stores generate 37.2% of revenue**, Neighborhood 30.5%, High Street 24.6% and Outlet 7.7%.

### 2.2 Category performance
| Category | Share of revenue | Return rate (units) |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Electronics | 20.7% | 0.60% |
| Beauty & Personal Care | 20.5% | 0.64% |
| Home & Kitchen | 20.1% | 0.75% |
| Fashion | 19.8% | 0.72% |
| Grocery | 18.9% | 0.61% |

Home & Kitchen and Fashion have the highest return rates in this month.

### 2.3 Returns
- Return volume is flat across reasons: Quality Issue 57, Damaged 57, Wrong Product 56, Customer Changed Mind 52,
  Other 51, Size/Fit Issue 49, Late Delivery 46.
- Quality, damage and wrong-product returns (170 of 368, 46%) are controllable by supplier and fulfilment
  teams; "changed mind" and "size/fit" are not.
- Store-level return rates range from 0.10% to 1.32%, so a few stores are clear outliers worth review.

### 2.4 Customers
- **About 1,045 orders belong to a blank `customer_id` (guest/anonymous purchases)**, and that single row is
  classified "VIP Tier" in the customer summary. Guest sales must be excluded from segmentation.
- Of 1,306 customer rows, 1,206 are "VIP Tier" and hold 98.6% of lifetime spend. The segment thresholds are
  not separating customers.

## 4. Recommendations

### 4.1 Business recommendations (valid to carry forward)
1. **Protect the Mall format and the South region**, which together carry the largest revenue shares, by
   prioritising stock availability and staffing there.
2. **Investigate East** for a store-level cause before deciding on promotion or format changes; confirm with
   the full history whether the gap is persistent (Doc 03 says it should dip further in Apr-Sep 2025).
3. **Reduce controllable returns** (quality, damaged, wrong product: 46% of returns) with supplier quality
   checks and pick/pack verification; target the stores at the top of the 0.10%-1.32% return-rate range first.
4. **Treat guest purchases as their own segment** and encourage sign-up (loyalty or e-receipt offers) so more
   revenue can be tied to known customers.

### 4.2 Project recommendations (to complete the deliverable)
1. Load all 32 months, the real targets (10,776 rows) and the promotions table, then regenerate every
   analytical output and this document.
2. Fix the cost model to use product-level historical cost instead of a flat 60% ratio.
3. Recalibrate store tiers and customer segments with thresholds that produce a real spread.
4. Explain or fix the roughly 16,500 January rows that were rejected, and publish the reject-reason breakdown
   from `04.12`.
5. Add the time-based analysis (MoM/YoY, seasonality, target attainment) and the hidden-event investigations
